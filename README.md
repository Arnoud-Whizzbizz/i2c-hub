DIY I2C Hub for use in fischertechnik Projects

Since I wanted to be able to use the I2C bus and various peripherals with 3.3- or 5-volt power supplies interchangeably on Arduino, ESP-32, or fischertechnik controllers, I designed this small I2C hub. Its features are:

-- Provides 5 and 3.3-volt power from a separately connected 9-volt input voltage to power the connected sensors and other I2C peripherals.

-- Connection options for Arduino, ftDuino, ROBOTICS TXT Controller, ROBO TX Controller, and TXT 4.0 Controller with their corresponding I2C signal levels via the familiar DC3 flat cable connectors.

-- Multiple Grove/Seeed I2C connectors for connecting sensors with a supply voltage and I2C signal level of 3.3 volts.

-- Multiple Grove/Seeed I2C connectors for connecting sensors with a supply voltage and I2C signal level of 5 volts.
